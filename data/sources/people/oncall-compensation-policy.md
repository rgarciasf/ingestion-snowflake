# On-call Compensation & Recovery Policy

## Stipend

- Primary on-call: $500 per week. Secondary: $250 per week (Lisbon: EUR 300 / EUR 150).

## Recovery time

- Paged between midnight and 6:00 local time: one comp day, to be taken within 30 days.
- Worked a weekend day for an incident or release: one comp day per weekend day.
- Managers must schedule comp days within two weeks; they do not expire while an incident is ongoing.

## Load limits

- No one should be primary on-call more than one week in four. If a rotation falls below 6 people, the manager must add a shared rotation with SRE.
