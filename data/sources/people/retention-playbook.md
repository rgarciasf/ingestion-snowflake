# Retention Playbook

## Signals worth a conversation (team level first)

- After-hours work or pages well above the team's normal for 3+ weeks.
- PTO not being taken (less than 1 day per person per month).
- A drop in the workload or intent-to-stay scores in the pulse survey.
- Promises made to employees (promotion cases, comp reviews, comp days, staffing) that are overdue.
- Recent voluntary exits in the same team.

## Actions that work

- Keep the promises you made, or re-commit with a new date in writing.
- Reduce load first: shared rotations, backfills, contractors, pausing non-critical work.
- Recovery time: schedule comp days and PTO.
- Career: promotion cases on time; stay interviews with key people.
- Pay: off-cycle reviews need VP and Total Rewards approval (see Compensation Bands).

## Guardrails

- Never use these signals for performance or disciplinary decisions.
- Survey data is anonymous and only shown for groups of 5 or more.
- No individual "flight risk" scores. Talk to people, don't profile them.
