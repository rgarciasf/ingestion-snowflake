# AI Token Plans & Overage Policy (effective September 1, 2026)

Pulse AI features (natural-language query, AI summaries, and AI Insights early access) are metered in tokens (input + output) per workspace per calendar month.

## Plans

| AI plan | Tokens / month | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Starter | 5 million | Included with Enterprise; $49/month on Growth | |
| AI Pro | 25 million | $1,500/month | |
| AI Scale | 100 million | $5,000/month | Volume discounts above 3 workspaces |
| AI Beta (unlimited) | Unlimited | $1,500/month flat | Grandfathered for design partners only, until December 31, 2026. Must move to a metered plan at renewal. |

## Overage

- Usage above the allowance is billed at **$80 per extra million tokens** on the next invoice. There is no hard cap unless the customer opts in.
- **The account's CSM must notify the customer at 80% and again at 100% of the monthly allowance**, and offer a plan change before any overage is invoiced.
- Overage above $2,000 in a month needs a CSM call before the invoice is sent (bill-shock rule).
- Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

## Shelfware

- Accounts on AI Pro or AI Scale using less than 10% of their allowance for two months in a row should get an adoption plan from their CSM before renewal.
