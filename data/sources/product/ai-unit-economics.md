# AI Unit Economics (internal, managers only)

## Inference cost to Acme

- Blended model cost: **$4 per million input tokens, $20 per million output tokens** (frontier model for AI Insights; same rate used for all AI features in reporting).
- Target AI gross margin: **70% or higher** per account. Accounts below 40% are reviewed by Finance monthly; negative-margin accounts need a pricing change at renewal.

## Watch list

- AI Beta (unlimited) design-partner accounts carry the highest margin risk: long-context AI Insights requests are output-heavy.
- AI Starter on Enterprise is included in the subscription, so its cost is a cost of service with no direct AI revenue.
