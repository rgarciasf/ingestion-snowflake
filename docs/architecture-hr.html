Ingestion · People GuardHR

The HR version of the app. An HR Business Partner asks “Which teams are at risk of losing people, and what did we promise them?”. The agent connects promises made in 1:1s and retros with workload, time off, engagement and performance data in Snowflake. Claude investigates and verifies, and retention plans are written back only after the HRBP approves. Pay-affecting steps are routed to Total Rewards.

User: HR Business Partner Team-level by default Salaries masked · survey groups ≥ 5 All employees fictional

Data flow: one question, end to end

Example: “Which teams show burnout risk right now, and which promises to them are overdue? Propose retention plans with owners.”

  1. Ask as a personaThe HRBP (or the Data Infra Manager) picks the HR version and asks. The browser POSTs to /api/ask with edition: "hr" and receives a live stream of steps, approval cards and the answer.
    Web app
  2. PlanClaude Opus 5.5 gets the HR mission, the privacy rules (team level, no individual flight-risk scores, no disciplinary use) and the schema guide, then decides which tools to call.
    Claude
  3. Find the “how much”run_sql reads TEAM_HEALTH: after-hours work vs the prior 8 weeks, pages, PTO taken, eNPS and intent-to-stay, voluntary exits, goal progress, the self vs manager gap and peer recognition. Each result gets a citation id.
    Snowflake
  4. Find the “why”get_commitments lists promises to employees (comp days, on-call rotation, comp review, promotion case), with status from the People Team follow-up. search_knowledge finds the exact line in a 1:1 or retro. explore_graph shows what depends on a person, e.g. Lena Okafor → Ingest Service → DI-4410 → Kestrel Bank.
    Snowflake
  5. Verifyverify_numbers sends the key figures to a separate Claude Sonnet 5.5 agent, which re-derives each one with its own SQL and returns pass or fail.
    Claude
  6. Propose and approvepropose_actions shows retention plans per team: workload, recovery, career, staffing and pay steps with owners and dates. Pay steps are flagged “needs Total Rewards”. The HRBP approves or declines.
    Human
  7. Write backOn approval, plans are inserted into ACME.BRAIN.PEOPLE_ACTIONS under the same role. Plans with a pay step get status “pending Total Rewards” and are never executed directly.
    Snowflake
  8. Answer and auditThe cited answer is streamed to the UI and the whole run is saved to ACME.BRAIN.AGENT_TRACES.
    Web app

Synthetic data: how it was made

From the real HR system

Only aggregate statistics: rating distributions, the self vs manager gap, review completion, goal shape, feedback rate, tenure and span of control. Computed inside Postgres, groups under 5 suppressed, no names, IDs, titles or text. Kept on the laptop, never published.

Fictional on top

187 people in 14 teams that match the original Acme documents and graph (Jordan Lee CEO, Lena Okafor Principal Engineer, Aisha Khan Product Eng). Salaries follow Acme's Compensation Bands policy, with tier 2 and Lisbon adjustments.

Guardrails

What the agent will not do

  • Score individuals as “flight risks” or profile people
  • Suggest performance or disciplinary action from these signals
  • Report survey or rating aggregates for groups under 5
  • Execute pay changes (they go to Total Rewards)

What it does instead

  • Works at team level and names a person only when a promise names them
  • Cites every number to a query and every claim to a note
  • Has every figure re-checked by an independent verifier
  • Waits for the HRBP's approval and logs every run
Same pattern as the AT&T HR talk in Granola: risk-tiered actions (pay steps paused for manual approval), scoped access per call (the persona's Snowflake role), and an audit trail for every agent step.
